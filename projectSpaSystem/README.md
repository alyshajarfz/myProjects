## SPA MANAGEMENT SYSTEM 

The Luxe Glow Spa Management System efficiently displays customer reservation records by retrieving data from stored files using Java. The system presents detailed customer information including name, phone number, membership status, chosen spa package, reservation type, vouchers, pricing, discounts and total payment. This feature streamlines data access and helps staff manage bookings effectively.

System functions include:

    - Tracking Total Weekly Sales: The system calculates total sales over the week and verifies whether the sales target has been met, ensuring the spa’s financial goals are monitored and achieved.
    - Analyzing Service Demand: It counts the frequency of each spa package selected by customers during the week, providing valuable insights into popular services.
    - Identifying the Most Popular Package: By determining the package with the highest demand, the spa can optimize inventory and tailor services to customer preferences.
    - Updating Customer Information: Staff can view and update customer details, with before-and-after comparisons to maintain accurate and up-to-date records.

Overall, the Luxe Glow Spa Management System enhances operational efficiency by automating data retrieval and analysis, helping the spa meet business goals and improve customer satisfaction.
