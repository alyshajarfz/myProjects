## FOOD ORDERING SYSTEM 

The homepage of eMenu Taste Haven Cafe features a scrollable interface, enabling customers to smoothly navigate through various sections. 

Including:

    - Home
    - Menu
    - About Us
    - Contact
    - Reviews

This layout offers a comprehensive and user-friendly overview of the cafe's offerings and story.

When customers click on the Profile link, they are automatically directed to their personal profile page, where they can conveniently update details such as their name, email and phone number to keep their information up to date.
